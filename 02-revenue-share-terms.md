# Foundry — Expert Revenue-Share Terms

**DISCUSSION DRAFT — not a signed agreement, not legal advice.**
Both sides should get independent legal advice before signing anything
binding. Written in plain language on purpose: if it needs a lawyer to
understand, it's too complicated for a first deal.

---

## The deal in one paragraph

Maxamad Industries builds and hosts a cited AI agent from the Expert's
book or materials, at zero cost to the Expert. If the agent earns money,
the Expert keeps 70% and Maxamad keeps 30%. The Expert keeps 100% of
their intellectual property. Either side can walk away with 30 days'
notice.

## 1. What Maxamad does

- Ingests the Expert's materials and builds their AI agent on Foundry.
- Hosts the agent on the public Foundry site with the Expert's name and
  branding on their agent page.
- Provides the Expert a shareable chat link for their audience.
- Maintains the service (hosting, updates, security) at Maxamad's cost.
- Gives the Expert a 30-minute review before launch — nothing goes live
  under the Expert's name without their approval.

## 2. What the Expert provides

- Their book, manuscript, or materials as a digital file (PDF, DOCX, or
  Markdown).
- Confirmation they own the rights to the material (or have the right to
  license it).
- Approval of the agent before it goes public.

## 3. Money

- **Split:** 70% Expert / 30% Maxamad, of **net revenue** — that's what's
  left after payment-processor fees (e.g., Stripe's cut). No other
  deductions.
- **Cost to Expert:** $0. Build, hosting, and maintenance are Maxamad's
  expense, always.
- **Pricing:** the Expert sets the price of paid access to their agent
  (or keeps it free — their call).
- **Payout:** monthly, by e-transfer, with a one-page revenue statement
  showing gross, fees, and the split. First payout 30 days after the
  first paid transaction.
- **No guarantees:** nobody promises any level of earnings. The agent is
  a product; products have to find buyers.

## 4. Intellectual property

- The Expert keeps **100% ownership** of their book, materials, brand,
  and name. Nothing transfers.
- The Expert grants Maxamad a **non-exclusive license** to ingest, host,
  and operate the agent — only for running this service. Maxamad may not
  sell, sublicense, or republish the Expert's material outside the agent.
- When the agreement ends, the license ends. The agent comes down (see §6).

## 5. Honest limits (said out loud, in writing)

- The v1 agent answers by **quoting the Expert's own material** — it does
  not write new advice, and it says "that's outside my training material"
  when the material doesn't cover a question.
- It is software, not the Expert. The agent page states this clearly.
- Maxamad does not guarantee uptime figures, visitor numbers, or revenue.

## 6. Term and termination

- **Term:** month-to-month, starting on the launch date.
- **Termination:** either side ends it with **30 days' written notice**
  (email counts), for any reason or no reason.
- **After termination:** the agent is taken down within 7 days; a final
  revenue statement and payout follows within 30 days; the Expert may
  request a copy of their ingested materials and any exportable data.

## 7. The small print that matters

- **Liability:** each side is responsible for their own content — the
  Expert for what's in their book, Maxamad for the software working as
  described. Neither side takes on the other's liabilities.
- **Disputes:** talk first, like adults. If that fails, the courts of
  Ontario apply.
- **Changes:** any change to these terms needs both sides' written
  agreement. No silent updates.
