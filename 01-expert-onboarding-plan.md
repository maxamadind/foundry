# Foundry Flagship Launch — Expert Onboarding Plan

**Status: DRAFT — preparation only. Nothing here has been sent to anyone.**
**Flagship decision: 2026-10-04. Guled: "Foundry I love it."**

## The goal

Three founding experts onboarded free on revenue-share. Their books become
live, cited AI agents. Each one is proof the model works — and each one's
audience becomes Foundry's distribution.

## The three target profiles

Not named individuals — profiles. Guled approves each actual name before
any contact.

**Expert 1 — The published author.**
Has a real book and a real audience (newsletter, social following, speaking).
Their book sits on shelves; an agent makes it answer questions 24/7. They
understand "my book, working for me while I sleep" instantly.

**Expert 2 — The niche coach or consultant.**
Sells expertise by the hour (business coach, fitness coach, marketing
consultant). Already answers the same 20 questions every week. An agent of
their method is a product they can sell instead of hours — and a lead magnet
for their high-ticket work.

**Expert 3 — The educator or trainer.**
Has curriculum, manuals, or a course. Students ask the same questions;
support eats their week. An agent trained on their material is a teaching
assistant that quotes their own words back, never improvises.

## Where they come from (in order)

1. **Warm first.** People Guled already knows who fit a profile. Warm beats
   cold every time — the first three should be conversations, not campaigns.
2. **The outreach machine.** The 740 drafted emails already target businesses
   with real problems; expert profiles can be filtered from the same lists.
3. **Targeted outreach.** Authors in niches with strong book-plus-audience
   economics (health, business, finance, parenting, trades).

## What we ask of each expert

1. Their book or manuscript as a file (PDF, DOCX, or Markdown — whatever
   they have).
2. Written confirmation they own the rights to it (one line in an email is
   enough at this stage).
3. 30 minutes to review their agent before it goes live — they approve what
   their name is attached to.
4. Agreement to the revenue-share terms (see `02-revenue-share-terms.md`).

That's it. No money, no tech skills, no work on their side.

## What each expert gets

1. A live AI agent of their book, free — built and hosted at $0 cost to them.
2. Their own branded chat page with a shareable link.
3. A payment button on their page when they're ready to charge (they set the
   price; Stripe handles checkout).
4. **70% of net revenue** from their agent, paid monthly with a simple
   statement.
5. Full IP ownership — they keep 100% of their rights; we only license the
   material to run the agent.
6. A 30-day walk-away: either side ends it with 30 days' notice, agent comes
   down, no hard feelings.

## Outreach sequence

Three touches per expert, spaced 4–5 days apart. Every draft below is
**DRAFT — NOT SENT**. Guled approves each name and each send.

**Touch 1 — the pitch email** (three versions below, one per profile).
Personal, short, one ask: a 15-minute call.

**Touch 2 — the demo follow-up.**
Show, don't tell: the live Foundry demo plus a 60-second screen recording
of their book's title page becoming a chat interface (mocked with their
book title on the demo corpus — clearly labeled as a mockup, never
presented as their real agent). One line: "I can build yours for free."

**Touch 3 — the graceful close.**
"I won't follow up again — the offer stands whenever you want it." Leave
the door open; never burn the relationship.
