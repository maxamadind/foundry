# Your book, answering questions 24/7.

**Foundry turns an expert's book into a live AI agent — trained only on
their words, quoting them back verbatim, with every answer cited.**

---

## What it is

Your readers open a chat page with your name on it. They ask anything your
book covers. The agent answers with your exact words — the real passage,
the real chapter cited underneath. Ask it something your book doesn't
cover and it says so, honestly, instead of making something up.

It's your book, working while you sleep.

## How it works — three steps, zero work for you

1. **You send the manuscript.** PDF, Word doc, whatever you have.
2. **We build the agent.** Your material is indexed; every answer is
   traceable to your text.
3. **You approve it, then share the link.** 30 minutes of your time to
   review before anything goes live under your name.

## Why it's different from a chatbot

- **It quotes you.** Every factual answer is a verbatim passage from your
  book — programmatically verified, not paraphrased, never invented.
- **It cites the source.** Document and chapter under every answer, so
  readers can check.
- **It abstains honestly.** Outside your material, it says so — it never
  improvises advice in your name.
- **It costs nothing to run.** No per-question fees, no API bills. Your
  agent answers its ten-thousandth question for the same $0 as its first.

## The money story

- **Free to build.** No setup cost, no monthly fee, ever.
- **You set the price.** Free for your audience, or paid access — your call.
  Checkout is handled by Stripe; a payment button appears on your page.
- **You keep 70%** of net revenue, paid monthly with a plain-English
  statement. We keep 30% for building and hosting.
- **You keep 100% of your IP.** We license your material only to run the
  agent. Walk away with 30 days' notice and it comes down.

Your manuscript. Confirmation you own the rights. Thirty minutes to review
